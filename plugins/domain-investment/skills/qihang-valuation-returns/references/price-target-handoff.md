# Price Target and Decision-Range Handoff

Consume the audited output from `qihang-financial-model-builder` before calculating or interpreting a valuation. Do not begin with placeholder forecasts.

## Method Reconciliation

- State which methods are applicable: DCF, public comps, precedent transactions, LBO, or another named method.
- Explain why each method is included, excluded, or assigned a weight; do not average methods mechanically.
- Reconcile enterprise value to equity value with dated debt, cash, preferred claims, and diluted shares.
- For public equities, state the valuation date, current price, price target, target horizon, and the named LTM or NTM metric used. For private deals, provide entry value and returns range rather than inventing a per-share target.

## Scenario Discipline

Show bull, base, and bear cases with the assumptions that change: growth, margin, capital intensity, WACC or exit multiple, leverage, and timing as applicable. Keep outputs traceable to the model handoff and identify the one or two assumptions that dominate the range.

## Required Handoff

```text
Valuation date and currency:
Methods considered and weights:
Enterprise-value outputs by case:
Equity-value bridge:
Price target or decision range:
Upside / downside and time horizon:
Key sensitivities:
Method limitations:
```
