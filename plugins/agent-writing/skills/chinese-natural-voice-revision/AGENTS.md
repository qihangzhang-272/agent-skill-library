# Chinese Natural Voice Revision

Use this instruction pack when a user asks you to revise a Chinese draft that feels generic, template-like, over-polished, AI-like, or detached from the author's real voice. Typical tasks include Chinese essays, reading responses, course reflections, personal statements, speeches, and short formal prose.

## Core Rule

Treat requests such as "降低AI味", "降AIGC", "更像人写的", or "自然一点" as authenticity revision: make the draft more specific, grounded, and faithful to the author. Do not treat the task as detector evasion.

## Hard Boundaries

- Do not promise that the draft will pass any AI detector or reach a target score.
- Do not intentionally add typos, grammar mistakes, fake awkwardness, fake metadata, fake citations, or fake experiences.
- Do not invent personal details. If the draft needs lived detail, ask the user for their real experience.
- Do not copy concrete examples from another user's old essay into the current user's essay.
- Do not destroy structure just to make the text look irregular.

## Workflow

1. Identify constraints: genre, audience, word count, format, whether to preserve structure, and whether the user wants comments, a rewrite, or a file.
2. Diagnose the AI-like parts:
   - repeated "观点 -> 感悟 -> 升华" paragraph loops;
   - stock transitions such as "由此可见", "这让我意识到", "综上所述";
   - too many abstract nouns such as "责任", "担当", "意义", "价值", "信仰";
   - overly balanced parallel sentences;
   - endings that sound like slogans;
   - claims without concrete scenes, actions, hesitation, mistakes, or changes.
3. If personal material is missing, ask 3-5 targeted questions before rewriting the core paragraphs:
   - "你有没有一个具体经历能说明这个观点？"
   - "这件事发生在什么场景里？当时你第一反应是什么？"
   - "有没有一次失败、尴尬、犹豫、误判或后来补救的细节？"
   - "你当时具体做了什么？别人有什么反应？"
   - "这个观点能不能落到一次作业、一次沟通、一次活动、一次阅读卡住的地方？"
4. Once the user provides material, select only the strongest 2-4 details. Do not average-fill every paragraph with all available details.
5. Rewrite toward natural Chinese:
   - keep the original meaning and required facts;
   - reduce stock transitions and decorative summaries;
   - replace broad claims with concrete actions and reactions;
   - allow modest uncertainty or hesitation;
   - keep the prose readable and grammatically sound;
   - end on a specific action, limitation, or real change rather than a universal slogan.
6. Final check:
   - every new detail came from the user or is marked as a placeholder;
   - no detector guarantee appears;
   - the text still meets the assignment and format;
   - the result sounds like a specific person, not a template unfolding.

## Output Modes

- If the user asks for suggestions, provide "AI味诊断" and "修改方向".
- If the user asks you to revise, provide the revised draft and a short change note.
- If the user asks for a file, preserve requested formatting and create a new file without overwriting the original.
- For school or formal work, recommend that the author do a final personal pass and add one or two details only they know.
