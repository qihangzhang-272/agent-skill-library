# Price Target and Decision-Range Handoff

When an audited model handoff is explicitly bound in the inputs, consume it before interpreting valuation. Otherwise use only approved upstream facts and assumptions; do not begin with placeholder forecasts or invoke a model-building capability from this method.

## Method Reconciliation

- State which methods are applicable: DCF, public comps, precedent transactions, LBO, or another named method.
- Explain why each method is included, excluded, or assigned a weight; do not average methods mechanically.
- Reconcile enterprise value to equity value with dated debt, cash, preferred claims, and diluted shares.
- For public equities, state the valuation date, current price, price target, target horizon, and the named LTM or NTM metric used. For private deals, provide entry value and returns range rather than inventing a per-share target.

## Scenario Discipline

Show bull, base, and bear cases with the assumptions that change: growth, margin, capital intensity, WACC or exit multiple, leverage, and timing as applicable. Keep outputs traceable to the accepted valuation basis and, when present, the bound model handoff; identify the one or two assumptions that dominate the range.
