# Public Company Research

Use this reference when the target is public or when a private-company fact pack needs the same business, management, competitive, and risk coverage.

## Source Priority

1. First-party filings and investor-relations materials: latest annual filing, latest quarterly filing, proxy, material-event filings, earnings releases, investor presentations, transcripts, and product documentation.
2. Company website, customer cases, and pricing pages.
3. Competitor filings and product materials.
4. Credible industry, regulatory, and trade sources.

Record publication date, reporting period, and URL (or document page) for every decision-relevant fact. If a fact cannot be verified, mark it `not found`; do not infer it from a similar company.

## Fact-Pack Coverage

- **Business model**: products, pricing, revenue streams, customer segments, concentration, geography, channel, sales motion, and disclosed unit-economics metrics.
- **Management and governance**: CEO, CFO, and other material executives; tenure, relevant track record, board composition, ownership, and compensation incentives when material.
- **Competition and industry**: direct, indirect, and emerging competitors; differentiation, switching costs, distribution, market structure, market size only with a dated source, growth drivers, and regulation.
- **Risks**: company-disclosed risk factors, financial dependencies, customer/supplier concentration, technology, regulatory, and execution risks. Preserve the source's uncertainty rather than rewriting it as certainty.

## Contribution to the Fact Pack

Provide findings under the headings above, separating sourced facts, supplied claims, estimates, and open questions. Insert them into `02-fact-pack.md`; do not invoke product judgment, competitive analysis, financial modeling, or an orchestrator from this method. The current Host owner controls all later routing.
