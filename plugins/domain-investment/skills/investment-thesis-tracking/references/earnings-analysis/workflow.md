# Detailed Internal Method for Earnings Updates

This method operates only on dated materials already accepted into the Run or supplied for standalone use. It never schedules searches, invokes another capability, or creates a separate office document.

## Phase 1: Verify the evidence set

### 1. Identify the period

Record exact fiscal quarter/year, period end, release date, and analysis as-of date. Confirm them from the supplied company material. Calendar quarter and fiscal quarter are not interchangeable.

### 2. Reconcile primary materials

Check that the release/filing, remarks/transcript, presentation or supplement, prior guidance, and consensus all refer to the same period. Record source, publication date, covered period, status, and limitation.

Stop and return the evidence gap when:

- the release and filing cover different periods;
- consensus lacks a date or definition;
- prior guidance cannot be established;
- a key claim relies only on commentary without the underlying figure;
- the latest required period is not present in accepted inputs.

### 3. Extract the source table

Capture reported and comparable values for:

- revenue, growth, profit/EPS, gross and operating margins;
- cash flow, capex, cash, debt, and dilution where material;
- segment, geography, product, customer, or usage KPIs;
- current and prior guidance;
- one-time or adjusted items with reconciliation.

Keep reported GAAP/statutory values separate from adjusted measures.

## Phase 2: Analyze performance

### 4. Headline beat/miss

Compare actual to dated consensus and calculate variance. Identify whether the variance came from operations, tax, FX, share count, one-time items, or definition differences. A beat with weak quality may still weaken the thesis.

### 5. Revenue and mix

Decompose growth into price, volume, mix, acquisitions, FX, and timing where evidence permits. Compare segment/geography/product outcomes and identify leading versus lagging signals.

### 6. Profitability and cash

Bridge margin change through gross margin, operating leverage, headcount, hosting/inference cost, services mix, stock compensation, capex, and working capital. Reconcile profit growth to cash generation.

### 7. KPI quality

Check metric definitions and continuity. Explain retention, customer concentration, usage, bookings/backlog, pipeline, adoption, or unit-cost changes in the context of the thesis. A new KPI cannot be compared to an old one without a bridge.

### 8. Management commentary

Separate direct supplied quotations from paraphrase. Extract explanations, uncertainties, priorities, and changed language. Test commentary against reported data and prior statements.

## Phase 3: Guidance and forward implications

### 9. Guidance bridge

Compare new versus prior guidance and consensus. Calculate the implied remaining-period performance and identify assumptions for demand, price, margin, spend, FX, financing, or milestones.

### 10. Estimate implications

Record which accepted forecast assumptions would need to change and why. Do not update a hidden model. Any revised estimate used in the thesis must show old value, new value, driver, source/assumption, and affected valuation output.

### 11. Valuation and recommendation impact

Trace changes to the accepted valuation method and sensitivity drivers. State whether the evidence changes recommendation, conviction, decision range, or merely timing. Do not infer a new price target without the required valuation basis.

## Phase 4: Thesis update

For every pillar:

```text
Prior expectation:
New evidence:
Counterevidence:
Status: stronger | unchanged | weaker | falsified | unknown
Conviction impact:
Action:
Next trigger:
```

Update risks, DD items, catalysts, KPI thresholds, and exit conditions. Preserve prior expectations to avoid hindsight rewriting.

## Phase 5: Quality checks

- Exact fiscal period, dates, units, and source lineage are visible.
- Every beat/miss claim has dated consensus or prior guidance.
- Headline, segment, margin, cash-flow, and guidance numbers reconcile.
- Reported and adjusted metrics are clearly separated.
- New estimates show their changed assumptions.
- Thesis conclusions follow the evidence and include counterevidence.
- Unknowns retain decision impact, fallback, and revisit trigger.
- No search, hidden model, separate chart pack, or office-document workflow was introduced.

## Completion result

Integrate the analysis into `09-thesis-tracking.md`. If the evidence set fails the verification gate, return the responsible gap through the current Host owner instead of continuing with stale or mismatched data.
