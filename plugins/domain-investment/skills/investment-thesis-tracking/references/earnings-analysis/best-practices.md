# Internal Quality Guide for Earnings Updates

Use this checklist inside `investment-thesis-tracking`; it is not an independent review Skill or delivery format.

## Decision quality

- Lead with what changed in the thesis, not a generic results recap.
- Distinguish magnitude of beat/miss from quality and recurrence.
- Explain causes, not only variances.
- Connect operational changes to valuation and conviction without double counting.
- Preserve counterevidence and unresolved questions.
- State what would reverse the updated view.

## Period and source integrity

- Exact fiscal quarter/year, period end, release date, and as-of date are present.
- Release/filing, transcript/remarks, guidance, and consensus refer to compatible periods.
- Consensus and price/valuation inputs have dates.
- Every key number names its supplied source and period.
- Direct quotations are exact; all other management views are paraphrased.
- Reported, adjusted, estimated, and assumed values are visibly distinct.

## Beat/miss analysis

- Actual, dated expectation, absolute variance, percentage variance, and prior-period comparison reconcile.
- EPS/profit differences are decomposed into operating, tax, FX, share-count, and one-off effects when material.
- Segment and KPI outcomes explain the headline rather than repeat it.
- Changes in metric definitions are flagged and bridged.

## Guidance and estimates

- Current guidance is compared with prior guidance and consensus.
- Implied remaining-period performance is tested for plausibility.
- Old and proposed assumptions are shown for every estimate change.
- No hidden model update or unsupported price target is introduced.
- Bull/base/bear implications use real drivers, not arbitrary labels.

## Thesis and monitoring

- Every affected pillar shows prior expectation, new evidence, counterevidence, status, action, and next trigger.
- DD gaps are not converted into facts.
- KPI thresholds retain stable definitions and baselines.
- Catalyst entries distinguish confirmed date, target window, and assumption.
- Exit/stop conditions are updated when evidence warrants it.

## Common failures

- Using stale or mismatched periods because the numbers look plausible.
- Calling a result a beat without dated consensus.
- Treating adjusted management metrics as reported results.
- Letting a headline beat hide weak cash flow, retention, concentration, or guidance.
- Repeating management explanations without testing them against data.
- Rewriting the original thesis after the outcome.
- Producing a separate report, chart package, or office file instead of updating the thesis Artifact.
- Hiding missing evidence behind confident prose.

## Final completion check

Before the node is ready, verify:

- calculations and tables reconcile;
- dates, units, periods, and definitions are consistent;
- source lineage is complete;
- thesis changes are evidence-backed;
- counterevidence and uncertainty are visible;
- each unknown includes reason, decision impact, fallback, and revisit trigger;
- the update is integrated into `09-thesis-tracking.md` and no parallel conclusion set exists.
