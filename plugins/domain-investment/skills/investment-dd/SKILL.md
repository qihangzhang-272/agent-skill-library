# Investment DD

Turn unresolved investment claims into a prioritized, decision-linked diligence program. Workflow use has the same depth, traceability, and prioritization bar as standalone use.

## Scope

Design diligence. Do not perform the diligence, obtain documents, rebuild a data pack, repair upstream analysis, write the thesis or Memo, or invoke another Skill.

- Standalone: use the user’s case materials and existing analysis.
- Workflow: read `02-fact-pack.md` through `07-valuation-returns.md`.
- Write one independently readable `08-dd-questions.md`.
- Treat references as internal questioning, workstream, and normalization methods, not independent Skills, Agents, workbook builders, search flows, or schedulers.

If a required upstream claim, assumption, score, or valuation driver is materially absent or contradictory, tell the Host which responsible Skill must add what and why. Do not hide missing analysis inside a broad diligence question. For unavailable external evidence, explain in business language what is missing, why it is unavailable, how it affects the decision, the conservative treatment, and when to revisit it, then continue the diligence design.

## Internal methods

- `references/dd-checklist.md`: workstreams, status, severity, and sector coverage.
- `references/dd-meeting-prep.md`: management, expert, and customer questions with evidence follow-ups.
- `references/datapack-builder.md`: normalize supplied financial and operating evidence, reconcile conflicts, and identify missing support without producing a separate spreadsheet.

## Analytical obligations

1. Trace each material gap to an exact prior Artifact claim, assumption, score, veto, or valuation driver.
2. Use P0 for potential deal-breakers, P1 for valuation/term/mitigation changes, and P2 for confirmatory or post-decision work.
3. For every question state why it matters, evidence requested, expected source, acceptable proof, and how the answer changes the decision.
4. Cover applicable financial, commercial, product/technology, OSS/IP, security/privacy, legal/regulatory, team/HR, operations, customer, and capital-structure workstreams.
5. Tailor management, expert, and customer-reference questions.
6. Build an evidence-request matrix with period, unit, granularity, document/data, current status, discrepancy, and follow-up owner.
7. State risk severity, trigger, consequence, mitigant, residual risk, and any veto implication.
8. Define what closes each P0 item and what changes the recommendation.
