# Internal Structure for an Earnings Update Section

Use this structure inside `09-thesis-tracking.md`. It organizes analysis; it does not require a separate DOCX, fixed page count, slide deck, or chart package.

## 1. Decision-first summary

- company, exact fiscal period, release date, and analysis as-of date;
- reported result and the most decision-relevant variance;
- recommendation/conviction before and after;
- thesis pillars strengthened, weakened, falsified, or still unknown;
- highest-priority next action or watch trigger.

## 2. Reported versus expected

| Metric | Actual | Dated expectation | Variance | Prior period | Source/date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Separate reported and adjusted measures. Include only metrics that matter to the case and keep definitions consistent.

## 3. Operating drivers

Cover revenue/mix, segment/geography/product, customer or usage metrics, gross margin, operating leverage, cash flow, and capital structure as applicable. For each major movement, state driver, recurrence, supporting evidence, and counterevidence.

## 4. Guidance and forward setup

| Metric | Prior guidance | New guidance | Consensus | Implied change | Key assumption |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Explain changed language, range width, implied next-period performance, and execution dependencies.

## 5. Thesis pillar update

| Pillar | Prior expectation | New evidence | Counterevidence | Status | Action / next trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Do not rewrite the original expectation. Unanswered DD items remain visibly unresolved.

## 6. Valuation and estimate implication

Show only changes supported by the accepted valuation basis:

- affected operating assumption;
- old versus proposed value;
- evidence or labeled assumption;
- impact on scenario/decision range;
- sensitivity and remaining limitation.

## 7. Risk, catalyst, and monitoring update

Record new or changed risks, catalyst dates/windows, KPI thresholds, financing/governance concerns, and exit or stop-tracking conditions.

## 8. Sources and gaps

List exact supplied materials, dates, covered periods, Artifact revisions, missing items, decision impacts, fallbacks, and revisit triggers.

## Writing standard

Use insight-led headings, concise tables, exact dates, defined units, and explicit causality. Quotations must be verbatim from supplied material; otherwise paraphrase and label the source. Avoid chronology dumps and unsupported management-language repetition.
